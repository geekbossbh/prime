Questions

Before you enroll

How soon do I get access?

As soon as your payment is matched to your reference number — usually within 24 hours. We message you on Snapchat and email you the course link.

Why do you need my Snapchat username?

The private members group and daily material live on Snapchat, so that's where your access is opened. Your username is only used for that.

What is the reference number for?

It's a single code shared by you and Lioness Prime. You add it to your PayPal note or Benefit Pay remarks, and we use it to find your payment instantly — no screenshots, no chasing.

Is my payment safe?

Yes. This page never sees or stores your card details. PayPal handles PayPal payments, and Benefit Pay is processed inside your own banking app.

I paid but didn't hear back.

Send us your reference number and the transaction number from your payment app, and we'll confirm it the same day.
